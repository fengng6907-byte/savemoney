# Save Your Story — Game Design V2

Save Your Story is a virtual life simulation that uses fictional expenses as prompts for real savings. It is not a real purchase planner. A player need not want a car, wedding, property or child in reality.

## Player loop

Choose an explicit personal savings goal, duration, country, goal currency and comfortable total monthly savings limit. Enter actual income and commitments solely to establish a safe game limit. Choose one virtual life, reveal a sealed incident, decide how to respond, see itemised virtual costs, approve an affordable contribution, save to a personal bank account, upload evidence, complete transparent local review, and unlock the next page.

The monthly 8th is Savings Day. Players may contribute early and in partial amounts. Future incidents are not presented as a checklist or pre-revealed timeline. Completed incidents form the journal; a sealed envelope stands in for the next unknown event.

## Two clocks

Story time follows realistic chronology in the fictional world, independent of actual time. A pregnancy can progress to a third birthday while the player's goal has its own real Savings Day schedule. Actual dates, income and employment never change through virtual time progression.

## Three distinct incident values

1. Estimated market cost: a country-specific item or service estimate, or a player-entered quotation.
2. Virtual expense: the part incurred in the story, such as a deposit, balance, covered repair share or selected quantity.
3. Assigned real savings: the explicitly selected contribution share, limited by remaining goal and approved period capacity.

An affordable partial challenge is never labelled as the full virtual expense. Completing a virtual purchase does not establish actual affordability or eligibility.

## Director and difficulty

The director considers remaining goal, remaining eligible Savings Days, comfortable capacity, current storyline, past choices, virtual assets, life stage, country pricing and experienced incidents. It selects the next chronological main incident or an eligible interlude. At most one interlude can intervene between main chapters. Candidate selection, probability checks and outcomes persist with the journey.

Difficulty changes selection, quantity, product / service choice, contribution share and schedule. It never changes an identical product's unit price to match a target. Different car models, policy scopes, repair services and replacement products have distinct estimates.

## Limits and completion

Monthly main-goal capacity is the lower of actual disposable capacity after the emergency contribution and the approved comfortable total less that emergency contribution. Currency differences use the player's dated MYR-per-SGD assumption.

A player may pause, resume, explicitly revise a goal or reduce a checkpoint with a recorded reason. A virtual incident cannot increase a goal. Essential expenses and actual income are never generated or altered by the game.

On goal completion, celebrate receipt-reviewed recorded savings, retain the agreed finish line, show virtual expense totals and decisions, and allow remaining story chapters with no additional mandatory contribution. A new goal is voluntary and receives no reused receipt credit.

## Visual direction

Cozy illustrated town, paper journal, warm cream, sage, muted blue, terracotta and yellow. Original SVG components show pregnancy development, nursery, child stages, car condition, rain, appliances, property handover, renovation and furnished rooms. English and Simplified Chinese; responsive bottom navigation; keyboard focus and reduced-motion support.

## Local-demo boundary

This remains the existing dependency-free static app. Evidence files live in local IndexedDB; progress lives in browser localStorage. The app does not access bank accounts or move money. Local evidence review is explicit player attestation with file / duplicate / allocation checks, not independent bank settlement verification.
