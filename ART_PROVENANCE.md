## Reproduction

The generated image is a production source, not a reproducible deterministic build. Future imagegen results may differ. `npm run build:art` deterministically rebuilds scene SVGs, self-contained posters, model sheets, manifests and storyboards from the committed JPEG settings and code. It makes no image generation request.
